# Landing page with working call (route /)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: rural citizens seeking government support, and the course examiner judging the product. Job: understand what Janmitra covers and why, then place a real voice call. Proof: the 18 published schemes, named on the rings; the working call screen. Constraint: the phone at the end is the existing VoiceCall component and must keep every behaviour. No assets or sentences from the reference site (user: "Not ours, layout only"); no political slogans.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The catalogue made visible. Five concentric rings, each a scheme category carrying its real schemes, then a scroll-scrubbed story that lands on a phone you can actually call. Refuses the civic template of headline over a row of feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Black ground. Five saturated ring bands, inner to outer violet, blue, green, saffron, rose, with a restrained glow. Anek Latin at heavy weight for display. Scheme names ride the rings as dark pills. White text; secondary text tinted from the ring hues, never grey.

STORY: The visitor sees what Janmitra covers, understands why help fails to reach people, learns that Janmitra listens in their own language, and presses Call on a working phone.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Rings centred high, the outer two cropped by the viewport edge. "Janmitra" at about 6rem inside the dark centre disc, neutral tagline beneath. A "Talk to Janmitra" action and a scroll cue at bottom centre; the action jumps to the working phone.

FORM: Pinned by the user, matching the reference layout closely: hero rings, three statements revealed on scroll, then the phone. seed: none (pinned) "I want the landing page like that" / "Match it closely". Signature interaction (user: "it feels as if I'm going inside the circles, like the circles are coming outwards"): a sticky stage scrubbed by scroll flies the viewer into the rings. They expand outward past the screen edges, outer rings faster as in a real tunnel, until the dark centre fills the screen; three statements then rise inside it in turn, and the stage releases into the phone section. Persuade-mode note: the pinned order puts the working action last, so the hero carries a direct link to it.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
